Representative Projects

A selection of work from Taut Engineering practice. No client names are included. Locations are listed where they add context.

Foundation and Geotechnical

Pylon Sign Drilled Shaft Foundations (Kentucky)

Designed two reinforced concrete drilled shafts for freestanding pylon signs, using LPILE analysis with the Reese weak-rock p-y model to justify reduced rock-socket embedment versus the presumptive IBC design depth. Included shear design under ACI 318-19 with the size-effect lambda-s provision, rigid-pier verification, and a full documented calculation package.

Mat Foundation: Jet Engine Test Stand (Nevada)

Reinforced concrete mat foundation and cast-in-place anchorage system for a jet engine test facility, including PLAXIS 3D finite element soil-structure interaction modeling and ACI 318 Chapter 17 anchorage design. Delivered as a complete engineering report with full calculation backup.

Structural Steel and Cold-Formed Steel

Backstop Netting Poles and Foundation (Ohio)

Independent verification and PE stamping of backstop netting pole and drilled pier foundation design for a school athletic facility. Included catenary cable sag analysis (H = wL²/8d), ASCE 7 wind loading, Broms and IBC 1807 pier checks, and a frost-depth correction that drove deeper shaft embedment than the original design assumed.

Freestanding Scoreboard and Video Display Structure (California)

Critical review and site-specific adaptation of a freestanding scoreboard and video display structure to CBC and ASCE 7-16 wind and seismic loads, using the ASCE Hazard Tool for site-specific parameters. Included drilled pier foundation design and a complete Excel calculation package. Sealed for permit.

AISI Cold-Formed Steel Hat-Section Calculator

Developed transparent, engineer-reviewable AISI S100-2024 calculation templates for hat-section truss members in compression (braced and unbraced) and tension, including Direct Strength Method checks for local and distortional buckling, effective width cross-checks, and ICC-ES ESR-referenced pre-qualified section properties. Delivered as a step-by-step Excel framework for client integration into a Python calculation library.

Pre-Engineered Parking Shelter Peer Review (Connecticut)

Independent peer review of a pre-engineered steel parking shelter, including an independent SAP2000 cross-check. Findings included a missing seismic analysis and an incorrect response modification factor for a cantilever column lateral system (R = 2.5 used where ASCE 7-16 requires R = 1.25 for cantilever column systems). Documented in a formal review report.

Solar Racking

Rooftop Ballast Calculation Library

Built ballast_calc, a Python library for per-module wind force computation and ballast demand for rooftop rail-based PV racking. Handles wind-zone classification, exposed-edge and corner penalties, deflection-limited ballast logic, and a pluggable coefficient backend supporting both ASCE 7 code-tabulated values and proprietary wind-tunnel GCp datasets (including RWDI formats). Built on Pydantic data models for validated, structured inputs and outputs.

Independent Engineering Review: Utility-Scale BESS (Texas)

Sub-consultant independent engineering review for a utility-scale battery energy storage facility, evaluating site civil design, structural foundations, equipment anchorage, and geotechnical reports on behalf of a project-finance independent engineering team.

Automation and Tooling

sap-composer: SAP2000 Input Automation

Built a tool that generates and round-trips SAP2000 .s2k input files from structured descriptions, enabling faster model setup for independent structural verification work. Produces documented, traceable model files that are legible and editable in SAP2000 without needing the generator.

Scoreboard Sign Structure Calculator

Built a per-panel scoreboard calculator with sign configuration inputs, corrected ASCE 7 wind load formulas, and a ledger beam design sheet, iterated across multiple market and configuration versions. Used to support a multi-project scoreboard PE review practice.

Plan Review and PE Stamping

Residential Structural Plan Review and Stamping (California, Ohio)

Review and professional engineer stamping of residential structural design packages produced by other engineers. Includes gravity load path, lateral system, and foundation interface review under IBC, ASCE 7, ACI 318, and AWC NDS. Delivered with a cover report documenting review scope and any qualifications.
