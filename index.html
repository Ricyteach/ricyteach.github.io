I'm a licensed professional engineer based in Dayton, Ohio, providing structural and geotechnical engineering services for projects across the United States and internationally. I work directly with clients, no layers, no hand-offs. When you hire Taut Engineering, you're working with me.

My practice covers foundation design and geotechnical analysis, buried structures and culverts, solar racking, structural steel and cold-formed steel, plan review and PE stamping, and independent engineering reviews. I also build Python automation tools and use AI assistance to run analyses faster, without compromising the engineering judgment behind them.

Expertise

AI-Assisted Analysis and SAP2000 Automation

Python tooling and AI assistance for building, verifying, and documenting structural models. Faster turnaround on independent checks without cutting corners on judgment.

Buried Structures and CANDE Finite Element Analysis

Nonlinear soil-structure interaction for buried pipe, culverts, drainage structures, and relining systems, including flexible pipe, GRP liners, and precast concrete vaults.

Solar Racking Engineering

Wind uplift, ballast design, and structural review for rooftop, carport, and ground-mount PV racking systems, including custom Python ballast calculation tooling.

Structural Engineering and Plan Review

Hot-rolled and cold-formed steel design, reinforced concrete, independent peer review, and PE stamping with a documented cover-report practice.

Geotechnical Engineering and PLAXIS FEA

Finite element analysis for foundation design, soil-structure interaction, and lateral pile analysis using PLAXIS 2D/3D with Python API automation.

Representative Work

Mat Foundation and Anchorage: Jet Engine Test Stand

Reinforced concrete mat foundation and cast-in-place anchorage system for a jet engine test facility in Nevada. Included PLAXIS 3D finite element modeling and ACI 318 anchorage design, with a full engineering report.

Independent Engineering: Utility-Scale BESS

Independent engineering review for a utility-scale battery energy storage system in Texas. Sub-consultant role evaluating structural and geotechnical aspects of the facility design.

Large-Diameter Pipe Deflection Analysis

Deflection assessment of large-diameter HDPE pipe for a municipal wastewater treatment project, including evaluation of long-term soil-structure interaction and installation-stage performance.

Backstop Netting Foundation: School Athletic Facility

Pole and foundation design for a backstop netting system at a high school, including wind loading analysis, catenary cable sag, and drilled shaft foundation design.

Structural Plan Review and PE Stamping

Thorough review and professional engineer stamping of structural design packages produced by other engineers, including commercial and residential projects across multiple states.
