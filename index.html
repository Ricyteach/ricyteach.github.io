01 / About

I'm a licensed professional engineer based in Dayton, Ohio, providing structural and geotechnical engineering services for projects across the United States and internationally. I work directly with clients, no layers, no hand-offs. When you hire Taut Engineering, you're working with me.

My practice covers foundation design and geotechnical analysis, buried structures and culverts, solar racking, structural steel and cold-formed steel, shop drawing review, condition assessment of existing structures, plan review and PE stamping, and independent engineering reviews. I also build Python automation tools and use AI assistance to run analyses faster, without compromising the engineering judgment behind them.

02 / Expertise

AI-Assisted FEA and Analysis Automation

Python tooling and AI assistance for building, verifying, and documenting structural models. Faster turnaround on independent checks without cutting corners on judgment.

Buried Structures and CANDE Finite Element Analysis

Nonlinear soil-structure interaction for buried pipe, culverts, drainage structures, and relining systems.

Solar Racking Engineering

Wind uplift, ballast design, and structural review for rooftop, carport, and ground-mount PV racking systems.

Structural Engineering and Plan Review

Hot-rolled and cold-formed steel design, reinforced concrete, independent peer review, and PE stamping.

Geotechnical Engineering and PLAXIS FEA

Finite element analysis for foundation design, soil-structure interaction, and lateral pile analysis using PLAXIS 2D/3D.

Shop Drawing and Submittal Review

Independent review of shop drawings for sports netting, ornamental metal, cold-formed framing, canopies, and equipment supports, with a stamped letter of review.

Structural Condition Assessment

Site visit, evaluation, and a stamped written opinion on existing framing, foundations, and structures showing distress.
