The role

What the Group Manager Does

The Group Manager oversees engineering operations in the Kettering office. That includes allocating workload, training and supervising technical staff, and supporting their professional growth. The Group Manager also maintains existing client relationships, develops new ones, prepares proposals, and executes contracts, and is responsible for project schedules, budgets, and client expectations.

  • Communicate with clients, contractors, and jurisdictional authorities for the duration of each project.
  • Supervise and review the work products of staff, including calculations and estimates, for technical quality and for adherence to schedules and budgets.
  • Forecast workload, plan staffing, take part in selecting staff, and give performance feedback.
  • Develop schedules and budgets for assigned projects and monitor actual performance against them.
  • Review project requirements, requests for proposals, and proposals.
  • Manage multiple projects at different stages of development.
  • Apply lessons learned from past projects to future estimates, and look for improved methods and procedures.
  • Comply with state and federal regulations and limit legal exposure and liability for the firm.
  • Collaborate with the other Group Managers and Directors across the firm's service sectors.
Qualifications

Required Qualifications

  • Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering, or equivalent experience
  • Professional Engineer license
  • Eight or more years of design experience related to site development
  • Four or more years of experience managing technical staff
  • Experience in business development and client interaction
  • Comprehensive knowledge of Autodesk Civil 3D
  • Strong organizational, communication, and computer skills, and the ability to work collaboratively with team members and clients

The work includes walking construction sites, climbing ladders, lifting 10 to 30 pounds, long periods at a computer, and occasional travel to offsite locations and meetings.
